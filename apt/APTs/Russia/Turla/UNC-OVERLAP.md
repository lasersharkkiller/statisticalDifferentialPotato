# Cross-reference — UNC5792 operational overlap

[UNC5792](../UNC5792/NOTES.md) was named in the **2026-06-29 US State
Department $10M reward** announcement and attributed to the Russian
**FSB**. Turla is publicly attributed to **FSB Center 16** (per CISA +
NSA + UK NCSC joint advisories) — both groups share parent service.

Turla's documented tradecraft is at the more sophisticated end of FSB
ops (custom malware families like ComRAT, Crutch, Kazuar; long-running
espionage targeting government / diplomatic / research targets). UNC5792's
article-disclosed Signal-account-takeover tradecraft is operationally
simpler and may represent a different FSB cell rather than the same
cluster under another label. Worth tracking adjacency anyway because
UNC-numbered Mandiant clusters historically fold into named groups
when attribution matures (UNC2452 → Cozy Bear).

**Tracking implication:** if UNC5792 IOCs later surface from CISA or
Mandiant, evaluate against Turla's known infrastructure for any
cross-cluster reuse.
