# Cross-reference — UNC4221 operational overlap

[UNC4221](../UNC4221/NOTES.md) was named in the **2026-06-29 US State
Department $10M reward** announcement and attributed to Russian
**Border Guards + military intelligence**. Sandworm (Voodoo Bear) is
publicly attributed to **GRU Unit 74455** — both groups share the
military-intelligence parent service.

Sandworm's documented tradecraft is **destructive** (NotPetya, Industroyer
/ Industroyer2, BlackEnergy-class wipers targeting Ukrainian critical
infrastructure). UNC4221's article-disclosed Signal-account-takeover
tradecraft is **espionage-flavored** rather than destructive — they may
represent **different GRU cells** with different mission sets (Sandworm
on attack/disruption, UNC4221 on intel collection from political /
journalist / activist targets). Mandiant UNC-numbered clusters
historically fold into named groups when attribution matures (UNC2452
→ Cozy Bear, UNC1878 → FIN12).

**Tracking implication:** infrastructure overlap with Sandworm is less
likely than with APT28, but worth checking when UNC4221 IOCs surface
from CISA / Mandiant — particularly any shared bulletproof-hosting
netblocks or recurring TLS certificate patterns.
