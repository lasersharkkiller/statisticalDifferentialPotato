## Description

UNC3661 (aka “Lapsus$” or “Lapsus Group”) is a threat cluster active from at least mid-2021 to late 2022 that appeared to be motivated by both financial gain and a desire for notoriety. While UNC3661 stole data from victims and attempted to monetize it by extorting the compromised organization, it also sometimes leaked the data for free. During their intrusions, UNC3661 relied mainly on stolen credentials to access corporate VPN environments and several publicly available tools and utilities, as well as built-in operating system tools. UNC3661 commonly installed or used native web browsers on victim systems as a means of tool ingress, and often conducted web searches for tools needed for the current stage of their operation. It also resorted to some attention-grabbing techniques, including public shaming, interacting with victims within the victim environment, and defacement attacks. In 2022, multiple individuals suspected of being members of Lapsus$ were arrested by British and Brazilian law enforcement agencies.
