# Cross-reference — UNC5792 operational overlap

[UNC5792](../UNC5792/NOTES.md) was named in the **2026-06-29 US State
Department $10M reward** announcement and attributed to the Russian
**FSB**. Star Blizzard's public attribution by Microsoft / CISA / NCSC
to **FSB Center 18** puts both clusters under the same parent service.

UNC5792's disclosed tradecraft (Signal messenger account takeover via
phishing of verification codes + backup recovery keys + SMS impersonation
of Signal support) is consistent with Star Blizzard's broader
credential-phishing pattern. Mandiant UNC-numbered clusters historically
fold into named groups when attribution matures (UNC2452 → Cozy Bear,
UNC1878 → FIN12); UNC5792 may eventually be merged with Star Blizzard
or one of its sibling FSB clusters (Gamaredon, Turla).

**Tracking implication:** if you run a hunt against Star Blizzard IOCs
and find adjacent unclaimed infrastructure, check whether it surfaces in
later UNC5792 disclosures from CISA / Mandiant. The reverse also holds.
