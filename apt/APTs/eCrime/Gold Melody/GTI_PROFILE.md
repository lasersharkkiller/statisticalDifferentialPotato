## Description

UNC961 is a financially motivated group that Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) has tracked since January 2018, with confirmed activity dating back to November 2016. UNC961 has targeted a wide range of industries based in North America; however, UNC961 has also been observed targeting organizations in Northern Europe and Western Asia. The diverse victimology is primarily due to UNC961's opportunistic capitalization on large-scale, highly impactful vulnerabilities in publicly accessible applications to gain initial access. The group uses both custom and publicly available malware in their operations. UNC961 uses multiple custom tunnelers including GOPASSAGE, HOLEPUNCH, and TURNSIGN. The group also uses custom backdoors including GEODEZINE, MUTEPUT, MONEYRUN, BACKSLAP, CHILLSHELL, DARKDOOR, HOLEDOOR, and RUBYDOOR. UNC961 has provided access to at least two distinct follow-on actors, leading to data theft and the deployments of MAZE, EGREGOR, and RAGNARLOCKER ransomware. UNC961 has also exfiltrated PII, network reconnaissance, and business-specific data from victims, although it is unclear what UNC961 does with the information. UNC961 has overlaps with Prophet Spider, a group CrowdStrike publicly reported on. UNC961 has been observed using language settings including Russian and Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), but GTIG has not yet determined a suspected location for this group.
