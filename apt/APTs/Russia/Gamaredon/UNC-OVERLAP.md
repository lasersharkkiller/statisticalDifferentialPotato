# Cross-reference — UNC5792 operational overlap

[UNC5792](../UNC5792/NOTES.md) was named in the **2026-06-29 US State
Department $10M reward** announcement and attributed to the Russian
**FSB**. Gamaredon is a long-established **FSB** cluster (publicly
attributed by SBU + CERT-UA, Crimea-based) — both groups share parent
service.

Gamaredon's documented tradecraft is heavier on endpoint payloads
(PowerShell stagers, custom RATs, file-stealers) targeting Ukrainian
government, while UNC5792's article-disclosed tradecraft sits in the
credential-phishing / Signal-account-takeover space. They may be
**separate FSB cells** with different specialties, or one may absorb
the other as attribution matures. UNC-numbered Mandiant clusters
historically fold into named groups when intel matures (UNC2452 →
Cozy Bear, UNC1878 → FIN12).

**Tracking implication:** infrastructure overlap with Gamaredon should
be evaluated when UNC5792 IOCs surface in CISA / Mandiant advisories.
