# Planmeca Oy Firmware Attack Surface & Detection Brief

**Scope & status (2026-10-27).** This vendor was on the "46 genuine OT vendors
still missing firmware" list and was acquired via **VirusTotal Intelligence**
during the 2026-10-26 acquire-and-analyze pass. The **behavioural differential
is complete** (see the section below). Full architecture enumeration, service
mapping, and CVE/advisory research have **not yet been performed** — this brief
currently carries the VT behavioural differential plus product/Purdue scoping
only. Anything not stated below is "not yet researched," not "absent."

**Census exposure:** 4 OT & Building-Systems devices across 2 states (per
[vendor-exposure-vs-coverage.md](vendor-exposure-vs-coverage.md)). A small
footprint, acquired for completeness.

**What Planmeca makes.** Planmeca Oy is a Finnish manufacturer of **dental and
medical imaging** equipment and software: the *Romexis* imaging / patient-data
management platform (a large **Windows** application), dental care units, 2D/3D
panoramic and cephalometric X-ray and **CBCT** imaging systems (ProMax, ProOne,
Viso), and intraoral scanners (Emerald). In an ARNG context these map to
**dental clinics and medical treatment facilities**. Because the software estate
is Windows-heavy, this vendor's differential is **Windows-only** (no ELF plane)
with a large suppression list — consistent with Romexis being a sizeable Windows
application that legitimately exercises much of the Win32 surface.

**Purdue layer mapping.** The imaging devices are **field/endpoint devices** on a
clinical network; **Romexis** is Windows **management/application software
(L3-equivalent)** that brokers image capture, storage, and patient data across
the imaging fleet — the higher-value Windows foothold, as with the engineering
tooling in other briefs.

**Known detection surfaced in acquisition.** `FireCRDriver_overwrite_20160112.exe`
(SHA-256 `28a9e6dc965bb37c9a7ce68c5772153ed462289be40c226591acd23dc7757a75`,
**8/72** engines) surfaced in the detections scan attributed to this acquisition.
It is a driver for **FireCR** dental computed-radiography X-ray scanners; it was
assessed in the IR notification as a **probable false positive** (an aged 2016
driver with a low, inconsistent engine spread) and flagged for IR verification
rather than treated as confirmed malware. Cross-reference
[PB-17](../../SOPs/OT-SOC-Playbooks/PB-17_Vendor-Impersonation-Trojanized-OT-Software.md)
and the IR detections notification.
